Sample Workouts

Three coached-style practices from the Columbia Bills (COLM) — about an hour each.

Curious what practice looks like before you jump in? Here are three sample workouts we swim regularly, each designed to run about 60 minutes for a typical masters lane — faster swimmers can add distance or tighten the rest, slower swimmers can round distances down or take a few extra seconds of rest. Actual coached practices vary by day, pool availability, and who shows up, so treat these as a preview, not a substitute for showing up and following your coach.

Distances are written in short-course yards (SCY), since that's our home pool at the Drew. Swimming long course (meters)? Use the same set structure and just swim the same distances in meters — the workout will run a little longer per repeat, which is fine.

@ :XX = send-off interval (rest = interval minus your swim time) desc = descend (each one faster than the last) DPS = distance per stroke, easy focus on technique
