**South Carolina Masters Swimming** is the US Masters Swimming Local Masters Swimming Committee (LMSC) for the state. Masters swimming is for adults 18 and over, from fitness swimmers and triathletes to national champions.

Join a club below to swim coached workouts, enter meets and open-water events, and compete for South Carolina at USMS Nationals.
